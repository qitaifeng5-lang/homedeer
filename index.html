04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things
you might be wondering.

Is Homedeer a real business?

No. Homedeer is a demonstration website created to showcase web design skills, responsive layouts and accessible interactions.

Are the services or Markham location real offerings?

No. Snow removal, leaf cleanup and Markham, Ontario, Canada are examples used for this design project. There is no real service area or physical business address.

Can I request a quote or book a service?

No. This portfolio does not accept real enquiries, provide quotes or schedule services.

What happens when I try the demo form?

Your sample entries are validated locally in your browser. Nothing is sent, emailed or saved by this website. Please do not enter real personal information.

What does this project demonstrate?

Responsive HTML and CSS, original SVG illustrations, mobile navigation, accessible form validation and basic search metadata.
