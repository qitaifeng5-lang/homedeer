04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things
you might be wondering.

Where does Homedeer Services operate?

We serve Markham, Ontario, Canada. Include your Markham neighbourhood in your enquiry so we can discuss your property and scheduling availability.

What should I include in a quote request?

Select a service and describe your Markham neighbourhood, approximate property or work area size, access and the help you need. Please do not send payment details or other sensitive information.

Does sending a request book a service?

No. A request starts a conversation. Service scope, pricing, availability and scheduling need to be agreed before a visit is booked.

Are ice treatment and leaf disposal included?

Do not assume these are included. Mention what you need in your request and confirm the specific work and disposal arrangements in your quote.

How will I know my request was received?

The form shows “Thank you! We will contact you soon.” only after Formspree confirms receipt. This confirms your enquiry was accepted, not a booking or a guaranteed response time. If confirmation fails, the form shows an error and keeps your entries.
